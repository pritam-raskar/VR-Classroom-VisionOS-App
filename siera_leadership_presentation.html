Part 1 · What SIERA is

What is SIERA?

SIERA is the AI decision-support service that assesses Level 1 sanctions alerts on payments and returns a recommendation with its rationale.

How it assesses

Rules plus AI

Deterministic MIERA and pattern rules handle configured scenarios. Eligible remaining hits go to an entity-specific AI agent through Eliza.

What it returns

Explained recommendations

Each hit gets one of two decisions, Inconclusive (HOLD) or False Positive (RELEASE), with an evidence-based rationale operations can review.

Who decides

People stay in control

GSU keeps review, resolution and escalation. A recommendation is not the payment action.

Key principle: SIERA recommends. The operational workflow decides.

Decision and Rationale

Evidence in — seven inputs

Transaction detailsPayment or transaction context
Sanctioned entity typeDrives agent selection
Sanctioned entity detailsNames, aliases, identifiers, geography
Screened field or tagThe part that generated the hit
Value in the fieldThe transaction value screened
Matched stringText or identifier of the match
Field or tag riskRisk context of the field

Message ID, system ID and hit sequence trace every result back to its hit.

Recommendation out — two decisions

Inconclusive (HOLD)Unresolved concern, not a confirmed match
False Positive (RELEASE)The evidence supports an entity mismatch
  • Inconclusive takes priority when hits roll up to the alert
  • Every decision carries an evidence-based rationale operations can review
  • Missing or excluded hits leave the alert partially reviewed
  • A successful model call or plausible narrative is not, on its own, a usable assessment

Part 5 · Future plans

Future Plans: Deep Research

Deep Research supports investigations that need additional evidence, and presents a traceable comparison to the Level 2 investigator.

What it retrieves

  • Incorporation and address
  • Ownership and directors
  • Business activity
  • Vessel identity or geographic context

Only through approved data access.

What the investigator sees

  • Retrieved information
  • Supporting findings
  • Contradictions
  • Remaining gaps

Missing or conflicting evidence stays explicit.

Where it starts

  • Company alerts first
  • Location alerts first
  • Other entity types as access, quality and operating requirements permit

Control point: a statement in an AI narrative does not establish that information was retrieved or independently corroborated.

Part 8 · QA Digital Worker

What is the QA Digital Worker?

A Digital Employee that runs on top of the daily dispositions made by people and evaluates their quality: did the screener make the right call, and how good are the investigation and the documented evidence?

AI-powered review

Persona-based AI agents emulate how a QA analyst reads, reasons and evaluates each sanctions disposition, at scale.

Human oversight

Cases the AI is uncertain about are flagged for a human analyst. The AI never makes a final call alone on ambiguous cases.

Structured output

Every review produces a documented rationale, stored in Snowflake and surfaced on dashboards, ready for audit.

Every disposition gets one clear outcome

AI reviewed (in scope)Needs human reviewOut of scope

Operations subject-matter experts provide the instructions. The AI follows their guidance.

Part 8 · QA Digital Worker

How the QA Digital Worker Works

Click a step title for details

Worker → Fixer → Challenger

The Worker makes the initial assessment, the Fixer verifies it, and if they disagree the Challenger breaks the tie. This Challenger belongs to the QA Digital Worker and is separate from the Challenger proposed for SIERA.

Smart routing

Dispositions are routed by rationale type (Individual, Company, Vessel or Other) so each review uses the right lens. Operations experts provide the instructions the AI follows.
